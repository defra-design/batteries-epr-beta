Add a beta banner and a Welsh language link to regulator pages

Change

Every regulator page now has a beta banner and a link to switch to Welsh. Both use standard GOV.UK components, so they work the same way as other government services.

Beta banner

  • Tells people the service is new. GOV.UK asks for a beta banner in private and public beta. See the GOV.UK phase banner component (opens in new tab)
  • Sits under the service navigation, with a link to give feedback

Welsh language link

  • Uses the GOV.UK language navigation component (opens in new tab). It's new (GOV.UK Frontend 6.5, August 2026) and in trial, so it may change
  • Sits on the right of the service navigation, as GOV.UK advises when the whole service is in English and Welsh. It's in the same place on every page, whether or not the page has a back link
  • English shows as the current language. Cymraeg is a link marked as Welsh, so screen readers say it in Welsh (WCAG 3.1.2, language of parts)
  • On a phone, it moves under the service name
  • Selecting Cymraeg adds ?lang=cy to the address, which is how the live regulator service chooses the language. Cymraeg then shows as the current language, and English becomes the link back

Why not the regulator toolkit's link

The regulator toolkit prototype has its own "English | Cymraeg" link, under the header and level with the back link. That follows the older DWP toggle to Welsh pattern (opens in new tab). DWP plan to move to the GOV.UK component. The GOV.UK component also marks Cymraeg as Welsh, which the toolkit's link doesn't yet.

Prototype only

  • The words stay in English when you select Cymraeg: there's no Welsh content yet. If you go to another page, it's back to English
  • The feedback link goes to a "not built yet" page for now
  • The words are in app/content/regulator/header.md
