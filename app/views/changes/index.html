What's changed

Interaction design changes to the prototype: what changed and why. Newest first.

Content changes aren't listed here. See every content change on GitHub (opens in new tab).

9 November 2026 New

Add the members page, in brief and full versions

Regulators need to see who the members of a scheme are. This is the members list only: each producer's figures by chemistry and category look like they're part of EB-366 (checking a submission), to be confirmed.

9 November 2026 Change

Move the Welsh language link below the beta banner

The Welsh language link now sits below the beta banner, on the right, on every regulator page. It was in the service navigation.

6 November 2026 Change

Add a beta banner and a Welsh language link to regulator pages

Every regulator page now has a beta banner and a link to switch to Welsh. Both use standard GOV.UK components, so they work the same way as other government services.

6 November 2026 New

Add the placed on the market submission and organisation details pages, in brief and full versions

Regulators need to see one scheme's submission for a quarter, who submitted it, and the scheme's details if they need them. There are two versions for the team to look at and say what we can build. We'd rather test one. Each version has 2 pages: the submission, then the organisation details.

6 November 2026 Change

Update statuses, overdue and empty cells on the placed on the market submissions pages

Content design updated the statuses and how overdue returns look.

5 November 2026 New

Add version 3 of the indicative obligation check, with the latest content

Version 2 was built to check our figures match NPWD, so it doesn't explain the obligation to a regulatory officer. Version 3 is a content design rewrite, for user research.

5 November 2026 New

Add the placed on the market submissions page, in two versions to compare

Regulators need one place to see which compliance schemes have sent their placed on the market data, and what needs reviewing first. We don't know yet whether they want one quarter at a time or everything, so there are two versions to test.

28 October 2026 Change

Split the indicative obligation check into three dated versions

At the 28 October playback the team chose the calculation table only, without the chemistry split.

27 October 2026 New

Add the indicative obligation check

Regulators had no page to check that a compliance scheme's indicative obligation in the new service matches NPWD.

27 October 2026 Fix

Fix accessibility issues on the indicative obligation check

Some section titles weren't real headings, the versions shared one page title, and the tables had no captions.
