What's changed

Interaction design changes to the prototype: what changed and why. Newest first.

Content changes aren't listed here. See every content change on GitHub (opens in new tab).

18 November 2026 Change

What changed in version 4 after the team walkthrough (EB-366 and EB-367)

Marie showed version 4 to the team on 18 November. Afterwards, she reviewed the whole journey against common usability principles and made a number of changes. This page explains what changed and why. The other change logs from 18 November have more detail.

18 November 2026 Change

Show 30 members at a time on the Members page (EB-366)

The Members page now shows 30 members at a time, with links to the other pages underneath. Before, all members were on one page. Recycling Lives has 281 members, so this meant a lot of scrolling. A keyboard user would also have had to press Tab more than 560 times to reach the end, because each row has a tickbox and a link.

18 November 2026 New

Add version 4: making a decision on a submission (EB-367)

EB-367: regulators accept, query or reject a placed on the market submission. Most of the time they go back and forth with the scheme before accepting, so querying is the main job, and a query is often about particular members. Designed and built on 18 November, in version 4, after EB-366.

18 November 2026 Change

Move the decision button to the bottom of every page (EB-367)

The Make a decision button used to be at the top of the Flags and Members pages, above the table. This asked regulators to decide before they had looked at anything. In Alex's regulator prototype, the Assign button works at the top because assigning is the main job on that page. Here, regulators need to check the submission first.

18 November 2026

Show the submitted date and how long ago, with Overdue after 28 days

Mak asked us to make it easy to see how long it's been since a return was sent, for the few EA users who review within 10 days. David confirmed regulators have 28 consecutive days.

18 November 2026 Change

Make a decision on one page, and see messages and notes on Submission details (EB-367)

After the team walkthrough on 18 November, Marie reviewed the whole journey. These are the changes to making a decision, and why we made them.

17 November 2026 New

Add version 4: checking a member's figures (EB-366)

Version 4 is for EB-366: regulators checking the figures in a placed on the market submission. It's a copy of version 3's journey, with its own pages and words, so version 3 stays as it was played back and tested. Start at the submissions list for the latest quarter.

Version 2 was built to check our figures match NPWD, so it doesn't explain the obligation to a regulatory officer. Version 3 is a content design rewrite, for user research.
