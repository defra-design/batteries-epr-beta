Add version 4: making a decision on a submission (EB-367)

New

How the journey works

EB-367: regulators accept, query or reject a placed on the market submission. Most of the time they go back and forth with the scheme before accepting, so querying is the main job, and a query is often about particular members. Designed and built on 18 November, in version 4, after EB-366.

The journey, in words

  • On Flags or Members, tick the members you want to ask the scheme about, then Make a decision. The member page has Make a decision too, with nothing to tick
  • With members ticked: Selected members, then the decision page. With none ticked: straight to the decision page
  • The decision page: accept, query or reject. Then one of 3 confirmations
  • No check your answers page: regulators have just made their choices on one page, and it's an internal service

Flags and Members: tick members (new)

  • A tickbox on each row (GOV.UK small checkboxes). Nothing ticked to start with
  • Select all on Flags only, in the table heading (MOJ multi-select, with JavaScript). Not on Members, where it would tick hundreds: to test
  • Make a decision (green button) at the top, above the table, so it's there without scrolling. Was at the bottom of Flags
  • Ticks save as you make them, so you can tick on Flags, move to Members, tick more, and they all come through. Anyone you've ticked stays ticked when you come back. Without JavaScript, ticks save when you press Make a decision
  • A line above the button explains the tickboxes: for content design to check

Selected members (new)

  • Heading: "July to September 2026 selected members", the same pattern as the submission's heading. It doesn't say "query": you can still accept or reject on the next page
  • Each member is a tickbox, ticked to start with. Untick to remove (GOV.UK checkboxes that reveal a related question)
  • The hint says why they're there: their flag, or their biggest change if they were picked from Members
  • Under each one, "Your question to the scheme (optional)": one line, a short nudge. It says it goes to the scheme, so it's clear where it goes
  • Continue, or Add more members, which keeps what you've typed and goes back to Members. Update list was dropped: Continue saves everything anyway
  • Long lists are fine, as with our long tables. How many members regulators pick at once is a question for research

The decision page (new)

  • Heading: "July to September 2026 decision". A line under it says how many members are selected, with Change
  • Accept the submission, Query 3 members ("Query the whole submission" with none ticked), or Reject the whole submission
  • All 3 are always there, whatever's ticked: regulators are qualified, and it's their job, so flexibility is better than locking options away. You can tick members and still accept
  • Not much in reveals: only Respond by, a date, under Query (UN-060). We started with a reveal under each option, and it was too much
  • Message to the scheme: one box, whatever the decision, always required. One rule, so nobody has to work out when it's needed. We tried "required only to reject or query the whole submission", but it needed a hint to explain
  • Attach a file (optional), whatever the decision. To align with the Rural Payments document upload: David is putting us in touch
  • The page is clear when you come to it. Answers only stay after an error, to fix them

Confirmation (new)

  • One page, its words follow the decision: Submission accepted, Query sent, or Submission rejected
  • A panel and What happens next, nothing else. Ian's alpha page also played back what was recorded (a summary list): we've done one, not both
  • No button or links: it's the end of the journey, and where regulators go next is a question for research
  • After a rejection it only says we've told the scheme why. What the scheme does next isn't known yet

Left out, and why

  • How the scheme is told (in the service, or by email or phone, UN-059): a preference set up once, for example at registration, not asked on every decision
  • Internal notes (UN-061): there's a need, but nowhere yet to show them again, and too much on the page. A question for research
  • Check your answers: see above
  • Refer to account manager (Ian's alpha): not one of our decisions
  • A date picker for Respond by: parked. First, find out whether regulators choose the date at all

Questions for the team

  • Who gets the query: the scheme's main contact, or whoever submitted? (Mak)
  • Do regulators choose the respond by date, or is there a standard time, like 14 or 28 days? (Mak)
  • How is the scheme told about a decision, and is it set up at registration? (Mak, Rich)
  • After a rejection, what does the scheme do? (Mak)
  • Attach a file: align with the Rural Payments document upload team (David)

To test with regulators

  • How many members do they tick at once? Do they want Select all on Members too?
  • Do they query about particular members, or the whole submission?
  • Does anyone tick members and then accept? Does that feel odd?
  • Is a one-line question for each member enough?
  • Do they need internal notes, and where would they look for them? Would they use a history of the submission?
  • After a decision, where do they go next? Do they expect the status on the list to change straight away?

Prototype only

  • The list doesn't change a submission's status after a decision
  • The file upload keeps only the file's name
  • Ticks and answers are kept until you select Clear data, in the footer: do that between research participants

New words are drafts for content design to review. The full list for Jen is in the Regulator journey doc, EB-367 tab.
