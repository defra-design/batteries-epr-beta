Show the submitted date and how long ago, with Overdue after 28 days

Mak asked us to make it easy to see how long it's been since a return was sent, for the few EA users who review within 10 days. David confirmed regulators have 28 consecutive days.

What changed (versions 3, 3B, 4 and 4B)

  • The lists' Review by column is now Submitted: the date the return was sent, with how long ago under it, for example "12 days ago" or "Today". Anyone working to 10 days can see it at a glance without counting
  • 28 days or more after it was sent: Overdue in bold, in place of the days
  • Once it's accepted or queried: just the date, as the status says where it's at
  • On the submission, Review by and Submitted are now one row, Submitted, with the same line under the date
  • Still what needs action first: the oldest return still to review comes first
  • Dummy data: reviews are due 28 days after a return is sent (was 10). Recycling Lives' latest return was sent on 23 October, so research sees one that's Overdue

Confirmed by Mak (18 November)

  • Overdue is just a label
  • The count starts on the day it's sent ("Today"); the next day is "1 day ago"
  • The count stops when the status moves on from Response received

To confirm (Mak is asking)

  • If a return is queried and the scheme answers, does the count start again?
