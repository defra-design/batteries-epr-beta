Add version 4: checking a member's figures (EB-366)

New

Version 4 is for EB-366: regulators checking the figures in a placed on the market submission. It's a copy of version 3's journey, with its own pages and words, so version 3 stays as it was played back and tested. Start at the submissions list for the latest quarter.

The idea: two ways in, one way to check

  • Flags bring the unusual figures to the regulator: each flag on the submission links to that member
  • Search finds any other member: on the member figures page, the search suggests members as you type
  • Both lead to the same member page, with their figures for two quarters side by side, by chemistry
  • No big tables of every member and every figure. Regulators check by member and by chemistry: in Round 2 they said scheme-level figures "mean absolutely nothing"

Submissions list

  • Version 4B (new, 17 November): every submission, with quarter, year and status filters, as version 3B, a back-up list for research. View submission opens the version 4 submission, and Back comes back to the same list and filters. A link at the bottom of each list switches between version 4 and 4B
  • One link per row, View submission. The scheme name isn't a link: two links in a row going to two places make people choose, and double the tab stops for keyboard users. Version 3 keeps the scheme name as a link, so research can compare

Submission: 5 pages with a sub navigation (new, 17 November)

  • The submission is now 5 pages: Flags, Submission details, Members, Changes and Scheme details, with a sub navigation between them (MOJ sub navigation), as in Alex's regulator prototype. Before, the details sat under the flags as an afterthought, and lots of flags pushed them down the page
  • Every page has the same short top, so nothing gets pushed down: the scheme, the heading and the sub navigation
  • Submission details ends with Files in this submission: the whole return as a spreadsheet (CSV), then the scheme's supporting documents, each with Download
  • Submission details has review by and status with the other details, and Last changed (the date a figure was last changed) with View changes. Was "Version 2 of 2": nobody could say what a version is, and what matters is that figures changed, and when
  • Scheme details (the organisation details) is in the sub navigation too, rather than a link from the Scheme row
  • Make a decision (a green button) at the bottom of Flags and of each member's page: accept, query or reject the whole return. It's where Rich's producer figures page puts it, and goes to his Make a decision page, the next ticket. Not built yet
  • Flags is the first page: what regulators want to see most. With no flags, it says so and how many members passed the checks, so you never land on an empty page
  • Flags are a table: Member, Flag, Scheme's reason, and View figures. No tags for flag types: the type is in bold words. Waste has around 10 checks, too many for tag colours to mean anything
  • Each page has its own address, so Back and bookmarks work, and it all works without JavaScript. Back goes to the list of submissions

Member figures (new)

  • Opens with the biggest change first, by size in tonnes, so what looks wrong comes first (18 November). Was name order, A to Z
  • Every member, with any flag types in bold under their biggest change (it's the change that's a variance, not the member), and View figures on each row (one clear action, as on the flags), and the chemistry that changed most on the quarter before, for example "Other: up 0.50 (42%)". By chemistry, not each member's total, so tonnage moving from one chemistry to another can't hide behind a total that stays the same. Sort by Biggest change to spot swings the flags didn't catch
  • Search by name or registration number: with JavaScript, it suggests members as you type, with the letters typed in bold (GOV.UK's accessible autocomplete). Searching filters the list: it never jumps to another page. Return searches, as in any search box: when a suggestion is highlighted, Return picks it, and Return again searches
  • Membership: current members first, or former, or all, as version 3

One member (new)

  • One page, in the order a regulator thinks it through: their flags (if any), their figures for two quarters by chemistry with the flag shown against the chemistry it's about, then changes to figures (if any): date, chemistry, before and after, who changed it. From Ian's amendment history design
  • One page, not a sub navigation like the submission: the flags, figures and changes explain each other, so regulators need to see them together
  • The member details are one line close under the name: a Current member or Former member tag, then the registration number, when they joined, and when they left if they have. Spaced apart, no dots. Was a Member details section at the bottom
  • Back goes where you came from: the submission (from a flag), the changes page, or the member figures list

Changes to figures (new)

  • Changes, in the sub navigation, lists each time the scheme changed a member's figures after first sending the return: the member, the date, who changed them, how many figures changed, and View changes, which opens the member's page with each figure before and after. The figures first sent are kept
  • From Ian's amendment history design (alpha), worked up with Gareth. Ian's design had a row for every changed figure, with the before and after. Here it's a row for each time a member's figures changed, so it still works when a resubmission changes lots of figures (waste will have a lot more)
  • Left out from Ian's design: regulator estimates (regulators don't edit data in the MVP), replies that confirm a figure without changing it, and filters
  • Comparing versions is part of EB-366 (Mak, design session, 10 November): regulators need to compare against the last accepted version (SV, Caroline)

To test with regulators

  • What do they compare first: last quarter, the same quarter last year, the last accepted version, or an average?
  • Does a biggest change on the member figures page help them spot problems?
  • Is a flag shown as two figures enough to decide whether to query?
  • Is knowing who changed a member's figures, when and how many enough, or do they need the before and after for each figure on this page?

New words are drafts for content design to review.
