Update the placed on the market pages after the team ideation session

Change

On 10 November the team reviewed the regulator placed on the market pages (EB-324 to EB-326) in an ideation session. These are the changes we made from what came up, and why.

Across all the pages

  • Flags is the umbrella word for anything in the data that looks unusual. Each flag has a type, shown as a tag: Irregularity (magenta) or Variance (turquoise). The word is always in the tag, so it doesn't rely on colour. Errors stop a submission, so regulators never see them. We'll add types as we learn them
  • The submissions list and the submission still have brief and full versions, to test with regulators. Organisation details and members are now one page each, used by both versions
  • "Admin" on every page (it was "Super user" or "Director" in places)

Filters keep their button

In the session, the devs asked whether the list could update as soon as you change a dropdown, added as a progressive enhancement where JavaScript is available. We've kept an explicit button:

  • WCAG 2.2, success criterion 3.2.2 On input (opens in new tab) (Level A). Changing a form control mustn't change the context unless the user has been told it will, and reloading the results counts as a change of context. Keyboard and screen reader users move through a dropdown one option at a time, so the page would reload on every option they pass. Public sector services must meet WCAG 2.2 AA, which includes every Level A criterion (accessibility requirements for public sector websites (opens in new tab))
  • Progressive enhancement. The filters are a plain form with a submit button, so they work without JavaScript and behave the same for everyone. Auto-submitting would mean two behaviours to build, test and support (GOV.UK Service Manual: building a robust frontend using progressive enhancement (opens in new tab))
  • An established pattern. The MOJ filter a list pattern (opens in new tab) applies filters with a button

Disabled until something changes? We also looked at disabling the button until a filter changes. The GOV.UK button component (opens in new tab) advises against disabled buttons: they have poor contrast and can confuse some users. Disabled buttons also can't be reached with a keyboard, and they don't say why they're disabled. Instead, the line above the table always says exactly what's showing ("Showing July to September 2026 returns: 4 submissions"), so it's clear which results you're looking at.

Submissions list (EB-324)

  • "Show", not "Apply filters": the quarter and year switch to a different set of data, they don't filter a list down
  • Brief version: Clear filters removed. It only put the defaults back, which was confusing
  • Full version: "Reset" goes back to the defaults, and no longer loses your sort
  • A line says what's showing, for example "Showing July to September 2026 returns: 4 submissions". It also explains why the submitted dates come after the quarter
  • What needs action comes first: the soonest review by date at the top, accepted last. Status sorts in workflow order, not alphabetically
  • Your filters and sort are kept when you come back from a submission
  • Still no pagination: there are around 4 schemes a quarter

Submission (EB-325)

  • Placed on the market (tonnes) on both versions: there was no way to see what had been submitted
  • Flags have their own section, against the data rather than a file, each with the scheme's reason. Specific words: "Up 42% on last quarter", not "Big change". The full version adds the figures
  • No data files (CSVs), Open or Download: the data is in the service, and the technical direction is not to store files. File trails and comparing versions belong to EB-366 (checking a submission)
  • Supporting documents stay: the scheme sent them to explain the return
  • "View member data" on both versions: members, not producers
  • Full version: BCS number moved to organisation details, a Version row like the brief version, and "Members in this submission"

Organisation details (EB-325): one page for both versions

  • View only, no Change links: regulators review, they don't edit. The details come from Companies House
  • No list of other users: that's account management, not reviewing a return
  • "Others who can submit", the number of members with View members, and when the details were last updated

Members (EB-326): one page for both versions

  • The full version: search by name or registration number, filter by membership and flags. One long list with only a search box didn't do the job
  • "Only members with flags" is a checkbox, not a dropdown with two options, and the column is "Flags"
  • "Reset" goes back to current members and keeps your sort. Membership sorts current, then former
  • The dummy schemes have up to 281 current members: the most any scheme has today

Questions for research

The questions to test with regulators are in Questions for research: placed on the market pages (opens in new tab).
