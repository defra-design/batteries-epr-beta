Add the placed on the market submission and organisation details pages, in brief and full versions

New

Regulators need to see one scheme's submission for a quarter, who submitted it, and the scheme's details if they need them. There are two versions for the team to look at and say what we can build. We'd rather test one. Each version has 2 pages: the submission, then the organisation details.

Both versions

  • From "View" on the list of submissions. Scheme names on the lists aren't links: the submission links to the organisation
  • The scheme name as a caption, and the status tag in the submission details
  • Submitted by: the name and role, with their email as a link. All emails are links
  • Organisation, after the submission details: the name with View details (page 2), and members with View members
  • Files, then View producer figures last: it's the main thing to do next
  • Back goes to the page before in the journey, worked out from the address, so it works without JavaScript
  • One label for the scheme's number, "BCS number". No approval number: it's the same number
  • Flags: the scheme's reason names the producer ("Brightcell Ltd is waiting for figures from its supplier"), so nothing is said twice
  • Dates in full in summary lists and cards ("23 October 2026"), where there's room. Short months only in tables, which never split over two lines
  • Left out, as agreed: conditions of approval, approved and expires, and account manager
  • On the Regulators list, the titles start FULL or BRIEF, so it's easy to see which is which

Brief version (Marie and Jen)

  • Submission details: submitted, review by, status, submitted by and the version ("2 of 2", with a link to earlier versions)
  • Files as GOV.UK summary cards, one per file: the file name as the title, with Open and Download, then the flags (just the scheme's reason, which names the producer) and when it was submitted. Cards stack up clearly when there are several files
  • Organisation details are view only: no Change links
  • People who can submit: just the people not already shown (the appropriate person and admin can always submit), each with their email and "delegated authority". No list of other users
  • "Admin" for the top of the scheme's account

Full version (Rich)

  • Submission details with Rich's extras: days left, BCS number, tonnage and producers in the file
  • Files in one table, full width, in 16px text and the MOJ scrollable pane: the file name (it opens the file, in the same tab for now), the flags (what the flag is in bold, then the scheme's reason), and submitted
  • Organisation details have Change links, so we can ask the business whether regulators can change them. People who can submit with their emails (if someone's authority has ended, they're not on the list), and other users in a details component
  • "Super user" for the top of the scheme's account

Questions

  • Can regulators change organisation details? (the full version's Change links)
  • Who can submit: more to discuss. A return from someone without authority shouldn't get through at all, so that's for the developers, not the page

Prototype only

  • People, addresses, numbers and figures are made up
  • Links to pages we haven't built go to a "not built yet" page
