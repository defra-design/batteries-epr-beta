Add the placed on the market submission page, in brief and full versions

New

Regulators need to see one scheme's submission for a quarter, who submitted it and whether they could, and the scheme's details if they need them. There are two versions for the team to look at and say what we can build. We'd rather test one.

Brief version: 1 page (Marie and Jen)

  • Submission details: submitted, review by, status (the tag is in the list), who submitted it and their role ("Tom Fielding, Admin"), and the version ("2 of 2", with a link to earlier versions)
  • One files table, full width, in 16px text and the MOJ scrollable pane: the file name (it opens the file: in the same tab for now, as "(opens in new tab)" made the names too long), the flags written out against the file they're in, and submitted (the same word as the list). Who submitted is in the details above, so it's not in the table
  • View producer figures straight after the files: it's the main thing to do next. The organisation details and members come after it, as they're needed less
  • The scheme's organisation details in a details component named after the scheme (for example "Recycling Lives Compliance Services Ltd organisation details"), view only. Emails are links. The people who can submit are in the list, everyone, however many
  • A link to the scheme's members below it (for example "Recycling Lives Compliance Services Ltd members")
  • "Admin" for the top of the scheme's account
  • From "View" on the brief list. Scheme names on that list aren't links any more, so two links in a row don't go to the same place

Full version: 2 pages (Rich)

  • Page 1, the submission: details with the status in the list and Rich's extras (days left, BCS number, tonnage and producers in the file). Then a short Organisation list (name, with View details, and members, with View), so the organisation is near the top but after the submission. Then the same files table as the brief version, plus the scheme's reason under each flag, then View producer figures. All full width, so the labels fit on one line and line up with the table
  • Page 2, organisation details: Change links, so we can ask the business whether regulators can change them. The people who can submit, with their emails (if someone's authority has ended, they're not on the list). Users who can't submit are in a details component
  • "Super user" for the top of the scheme's account
  • From "View submission" (page 1) or a scheme name (page 2) on the full list

Both versions

  • The scheme name as a caption, and the status tag in the submission details
  • Submitted by shows the name and role, and their email as a link. All emails are links
  • On the Regulators list, the titles start FULL or BRIEF, so it's easy to see which is which
  • Back goes to the page before in the journey, worked out from the address, so it works without JavaScript
  • One label for the scheme's number, "BCS number". No approval number: it's the same number
  • Left out, as agreed: conditions of approval, approved and expires, and account manager
  • The Action column is right aligned on both lists

Questions

  • Can regulators change organisation details? (the full version's Change links)
  • Who can submit: more to discuss. A return from someone without authority shouldn't get through at all, so that's for the developers, not the page

Prototype only

  • People, addresses, numbers and figures are made up
  • Links to pages we haven't built go to a "not built yet" page
