Add the members page (brief version)

New

Regulators need to see who the members of a scheme are. This is the members list only: each producer's figures by chemistry and category look like they're part of EB-366 (checking a submission), to confirm with Mak.

On the page

  • The scheme name as a caption, "Members" as the heading, and an intro line from Jen (to write)
  • A sortable table (MOJ sortable table), alphabetical by member, in 16px text and the MOJ scrollable pane: member (a link to their organisation details), BPRN, status (current or former), outstanding flags in this submission, and View submissions
  • Former members stay on the list for history: to check with Phil
  • From "View members" on the submission or the organisation details. Back goes to whichever you came from

Left out, as agreed with Jen

  • Producer size (only large producers join schemes), date joined, Download CSV, View scheme record

Later

  • Filters or search, if schemes have hundreds of members. How many members to design for isn't agreed yet (20 to 250, or 200 to 600)

Prototype only

  • Brief version only for now
  • Member names and BPRNs are made up, and the real BPRN format is to check
  • Member names and View submissions go to a "not built yet" page
