Patterns

The shared components we align with, and what we use from outside the GOV.UK Design System.

Defra Internal Frontend

A shared library of components for Defra internal services, such as regulator services, built on GOV.UK Frontend. It's maintained by the EPR Regulators team. Geoff Kaile is the front-end developer.

Developers build regulator pages from this library, so where it has a component, we use it, or something that matches it. When we need something it doesn't have, we tell Geoff.

  • Defra Internal Frontend: components (opens in new tab)
  • Defra Internal Frontend on GitHub (opens in new tab)

Regulator toolkit prototype

The regulator toolkit team own the shared design for regulator services across EPR. On regulator pages, we use their patterns and stay aligned with them. Anything new we design for regulators, we share with them.

Alex Colbeck is their interaction designer. Ask Marie or Alex for the password.

Small things we've added

Not components: small additions to make the pages work. Each is explained in the README, "Add-ons", with how to remove it.

  • Words files: the words for each page live in app/content/, so content design can change them without touching the code
  • Filters in a row: search boxes, dropdowns and buttons side by side, wrapping on a phone
  • A checkbox column in a table, only as wide as the checkbox
  • Remember a table's sort, when you come back to the page
  • Save ticks as you go, so you can tick on Flags and Members before making a decision
  • Record a change: the What's changed pages, with screenshots and journey diagrams
